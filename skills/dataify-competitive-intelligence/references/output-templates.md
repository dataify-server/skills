# Output Templates

## Concise Comparison

```markdown
# Competitive comparison — YYYY-MM-DD
## Executive answer
## Scope and assumptions
## Comparison table
## Key findings
## Evidence gaps
## Top three actions
```

## Full Report

```markdown
# Competitive intelligence report — YYYY-MM-DD
## Decision and scope
## Method and sources
## Market context
## Competitor snapshots
## Product and workflow comparison
## Pricing comparison
## Customer and review signals
## Recent changes and hiring signals
## Opportunities and risks
## Prioritized recommendations
## Unknowns and limitations
```


## Battlecard

```markdown
## When this competitor appears
## Their credible strengths
## Their exposed weaknesses
## Likely objections
## Evidence-backed response
## Questions to qualify the buyer
## Claims we should not make
```

Keep citations adjacent to material claims. State when each source was observed, especially for pricing, product availability, job openings, and other volatile facts.
