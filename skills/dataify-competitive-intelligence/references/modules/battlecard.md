# Competitive Battlecard

Required input: your product, competitor, buyer segment, and sales situation.

Build from current product, pricing, proof, review, and recent-change evidence. Separate the competitor's credible strengths from unsupported claims. Map buyer needs to differentiators rather than producing generic feature superiority statements.

Deliver when the competitor appears, credible strengths, exposed weaknesses, likely objections, evidence-backed responses, qualification questions, landmines, and claims sales must not make. Every response must trace to a source or be labeled as recommended positioning.
