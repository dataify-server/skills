# Competitor Snapshot

Required input: company or product name. Prefer a known official domain; discover and verify it when absent.

Collect the official homepage, product or documentation page, pricing page, about page, one current news source, and structured company data when available. Start with 3–8 actions. Record positioning, target audience, products, public proof, business model, recent changes, and evidence date.

Treat company claims as positioning evidence. Do not treat employee counts, customer counts, or funding figures from different dates as directly comparable. Deliver an executive profile, evidence table, strengths, vulnerabilities, unknowns, confidence, and recommended follow-up.
