# Market Landscape

Required input: market definition, geography, customer segment, and decision.

Run multiple discovery queries, then verify shortlisted vendors against first-party sources. Separate direct competitors, adjacent competitors, substitutes, and build-it-yourself alternatives. Segment by customer, job-to-be-done, delivery model, or pricing model before identifying whitespace.

Do not treat search rank as market share or an uncited vendor list as complete. Deliver inclusion criteria, categorized players, segment map, evidence gaps, whitespace hypotheses, confidence, and recommended validation.
