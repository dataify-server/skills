# Review Intelligence

Required input: brands or products, date window, and audience or decision context.

Use dedicated platform scrapers for protected or structured review sources and SERP discovery for accessible sources. Capture source, date, rating when available, text, product, segment, and URL. Report sample size and source bias.

Deduplicate repeated or syndicated reviews. Cluster recurring praise, complaints, switching triggers, and requested improvements. Separate anecdotes from repeated signals and avoid generalizing across customer segments. Deliver themes with evidence counts, confidence, representative sources, and product implications.
