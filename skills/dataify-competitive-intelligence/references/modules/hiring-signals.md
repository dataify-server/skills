# Hiring Signals

Required input: companies, geography, and freshness window.

Prefer official career pages; use Google Jobs, Indeed, LinkedIn company evidence, or Web Unlocker when appropriate. Capture title, function, location, seniority, posting date, and source URL. Deduplicate reposted roles.

Group roles by function, geography, and seniority. Describe hiring as a possible investment signal, never confirmed strategy. Deliver the role inventory, notable concentrations or changes, alternative explanations, confidence, and implications.
