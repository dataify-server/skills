# Pricing Intelligence

Required input: vendors and a comparable workload. If no workload is provided, state a narrow assumption before calculating.

Find official pricing and billing documentation using domain-scoped search, then retrieve the relevant pages. Capture currency, billing period, included units, overage units, minimum commitment, discounts, limits, free allowance, and observation date.

Convert every vendor to the same workload and unit. Show the formula, assumptions, exclusions, and sensitivity drivers. Do not compare headline plan prices when included usage differs. Mark sales-only or unavailable prices as unknown rather than estimating them.
